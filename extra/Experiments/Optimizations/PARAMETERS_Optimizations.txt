With this series of tests I want to find out what 'variables'/changes in the programmcode and/or environment have an effect on the throughput between two nodes.
Test duration: ~120 seconds

timer enabled

RX_BUFFER 2048
TIMER_PERIOD 1000000
payload size = 1459 bytes



Tests:

---
APv2_at home

test conducted at home

I assume that the network experiences less congestion due to lower density of people and their respective devices compared to the work environment of eth.
---
APv2_at home_no float

test conducted at home
float variable removed

Does the removal of the float var in the throughput measurement affect the performance?

Result: The difference seems marginal. I'll leave it out, bc. it's not needed.
---
APv2_at etz

test conducted in ETZ H68.2
float variable removed

This test is for comparing against "APv2_at home"
Laptop is in between the two nodes. Does this affect throughput?
